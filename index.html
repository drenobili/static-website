About

Computer Science, writing, and basketball meet through data and storytelling.

Andre is a 4th-year Computer Science student at the University of the East, balancing technical study with a parallel life in student journalism and basketball coverage.

He writes for The Dawn, the official student publication of the University of the East, and contributes to ALL-STAR Magazine, where his work centers on basketball reporting and data analysis.

Outside the classroom and the newsroom, he owns and runs @thefloorgeneralph on Instagram and TikTok — a platform built around basketball statistics, analytics, and data-driven content.
